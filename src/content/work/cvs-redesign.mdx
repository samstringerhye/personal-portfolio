## Design system summary and follow-on work

The shared rules let category content change without changing how the interface organized it. That balance between consistency and variation guided the work I shaped with the team, from the visual language to product selection and fulfillment. According to [project teammate Joseph Agresta](https://portfolio.josephagresta.com/portfolio_page/cvs-shop-experience/), the broader team’s work led to eight follow-on projects and helped establish Razorfish as CVS’s digital agency of record.
</ProseGroup>
