## The challenge.

CVS is a pharmacy and a retailer, and its shop grew through acquisitions: fractured type, mixed icons, one-off components. The site was live, so nothing could start over.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

I started with what every page shares: one type scale, and every icon drawn on one grid in CVS red. Pages are assembled from one library of parts.

</ProseGroup>

New pages are assembled from shared parts instead of built one component at a time.
