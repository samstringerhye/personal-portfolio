## The challenge.

People open a telecom app with a question: a bill, a technician, an outage. The app had drifted for years, with layouts that changed from flow to flow and no motion.

</ProseGroup>

## The approach.

The main action on every screen gets the most weight, and secondary actions sit in the same place in every flow. Every animation answers the user's question.

</ProseGroup>
