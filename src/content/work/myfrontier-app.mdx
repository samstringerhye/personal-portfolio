## The challenge.

Nobody opens a telecom app to browse. People open MyFrontier with a question about a bill, a technician on the way or internet that's down, and the app had drifted for years: layouts that changed from flow to flow, controls with no feedback, no motion.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

### One layout system.

The most important action on each screen gets the most visual weight, and secondary actions sit in the same groups from flow to flow, so people build muscle memory.

</ProseGroup>

### Motion that answers a question.

Every animation answers something the user is already asking: did my payment go through, is my appointment set, am I done. I built each Lottie in After Effects and handed dev the easing and timing.

</ProseGroup>
