## The challenge.

Nobody opens a telecom app to browse. People open MyFrontier with a question about a bill, a technician on the way or internet that's down, and the app had drifted for years: layouts that changed from flow to flow, controls with no feedback, no motion.

</ProseGroup>

## The approach.

The most important action on each screen gets the most visual weight, secondary actions sit in the same groups from flow to flow, and every animation answers a question the user is already asking. I built each Lottie in After Effects and handed dev the easing and timing.

</ProseGroup>

In four months the team delivered every core screen on one layout system, with four animations for launch, payment, setup and confirmation, each handed off with easing and timing specs.
