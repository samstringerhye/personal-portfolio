## Project scale and impact evidence

The P6 case-study deck reports that Release 1 reached 45 markets by June 2020, with later phases planned across 88+ markets. The October 2021 Samsung Global case study describes the broader rollout as underway. Those figures describe the team’s program scale; they do not measure the effect of my design contribution. My documented milestone was final design handoff to Samsung’s development team in 2021. The project records reviewed do not provide a post-launch user or business measure tied to my work.
