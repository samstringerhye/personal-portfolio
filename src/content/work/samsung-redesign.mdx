<ProseGroup>

## The challenge.

Years of market-by-market additions left the navigation with a different hierarchy, string length and workaround in every market, and right-to-left scripts flipped its layout. It had to read as one brand everywhere and flex enough that no market needed to break it.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

Cutting depth couldn't mean hiding products, so every category got a product image, from the desktop panel to the mobile drill-in, stress-tested against right-to-left scripts and deep catalogs. Regional teams got components they could compose without breaking the brand.

</ProseGroup>
