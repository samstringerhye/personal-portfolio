## The challenge.

Every market had added its own categories, string lengths and workarounds. The navigation had to read as one brand everywhere, including right-to-left scripts.

</ProseGroup>

## The approach.

A product image beside every category, from the desktop panel to the mobile drill-in, so shoppers spot a product before reading its label.

</ProseGroup>
