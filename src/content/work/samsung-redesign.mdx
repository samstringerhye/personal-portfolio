<p class="cs-intro">I was one of twelve designers who redesigned Samsung.com across 12+ markets in six months. I set the global navigation's visual direction, designed four core page types and contributed design system components.</p>

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The challenge.

Years of market-by-market additions left the navigation with a different hierarchy, string length and workaround in every market, and right-to-left scripts flipped its layout. It had to read as one brand everywhere and flex enough that no market needed to break it.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

### A picture beside every category.

Cutting depth couldn't mean hiding products, so every category got a product image, from the desktop panel to the mobile drill-in. I stress-tested each variation against right-to-left scripts and deep catalogs.

</ProseGroup>

I contributed atoms and components to Samsung's design system, so regional teams could build market-specific pages without breaking the brand.
