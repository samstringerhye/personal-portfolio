## The challenge.

The old app worked, but it didn't feel like Whataburger. Ordering took too many taps, the interface leaned on generic QSR patterns, and ordering, rewards and location finding felt like separate products.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

### Three directions, one merge.

I tested three visual directions against the same wireframes and merged two: Vibrant Bold's iconography, type and color on Modern's components and lighter palette.

</ProseGroup>

### A system before screens.

The rebuild, Pele, replaced brand-named primitives with 258 variables across three collections, adding interaction states, spacing, motion and elevation, named to map to React props.

</ProseGroup>
