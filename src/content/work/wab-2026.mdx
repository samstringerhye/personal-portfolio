## Lessons from color, type, and selection states

The project sharpened how I use brand elements in an interface. A color or shadow needed to communicate an action or a selection. A type change needed to establish hierarchy. I would bring those questions into the first round of exploration, before extending a direction across the app.
</ProseGroup>
