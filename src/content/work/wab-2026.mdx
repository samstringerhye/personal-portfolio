## The challenge.

The old app worked, but it didn't feel like Whataburger. Ordering took too many taps, the interface leaned on generic QSR patterns, and ordering, rewards and location finding felt like separate products.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

## The approach.

I merged the two strongest directions, then cut the order path: home leads with reorders and the nearest location, and customization shows only what's relevant at each step. Underneath, the rebuilt system, Pele, gives every screen 258 tokens named to map to React props.

</ProseGroup>
