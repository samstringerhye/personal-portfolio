## The challenge.

A Bespoke fridge means a model, a size and colors for four door panels. Online, every choice arrived at once, and people left.

</ProseGroup>

## The approach.

I ordered the decisions the way a salesperson would: products, type, depth, then colors. Each appears only once the last is settled, with the steps always in view.

</ProseGroup>
