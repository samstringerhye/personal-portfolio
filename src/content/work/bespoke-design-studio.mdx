## The challenge.

A Bespoke fridge means a model, then a size, then colors for four door panels. Online, every choice arrived at once and people bailed, as the early prototypes confirmed. In a store, a salesperson sets the order. Online, the interface had to.

</ProseGroup>

## The approach.

</ProseGroup>

<ProseGroup>

### A salesperson's order.

Each choice appears only once the last is settled: products, type, depth, then colors. Rounds of wireframes tested the order, and the user flow mapped every branch.

</ProseGroup>
