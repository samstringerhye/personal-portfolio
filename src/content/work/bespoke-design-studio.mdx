<ProseGroup>
## Lessons from guided configuration

Bespoke grew from helping shoppers customize one refrigerator to helping them configure a kitchen. I led the interface decisions across that expansion, balancing a guided first pass with the ability to revisit choices without starting over.

The central trade-off changed as the experience grew: a sequence could help someone complete an appliance, but it could also get in the way of revising it. My later proposals kept the model choice first and opened the remaining options sooner, carrying that tension into the next iteration.
</ProseGroup>
